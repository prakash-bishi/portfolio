# DESIGN.md — Visual & Interaction Design

## Direction

**Scientific + Cool + Clean + Professional.**

- **Scientific** — structured layouts, technical visual language, subtle
  data/AI references, clean information hierarchy.
- **Cool** — modern typography, refined motion, tasteful interaction
  details.
- **Clean** — generous whitespace, restrained components, clear hierarchy,
  minimal clutter.
- **Professional** — credible, mature, readable, trustworthy.

## Explicitly Avoid

- Childish design
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive animation
- Generic "AI landing page" clichés (glowing orbs, stock robot imagery,
  neon gradients on everything)
- Visual clutter
- Unnecessary 3D effects

## Non-negotiable constraints carried from PRD/RULES

- Must work well on mobile, tablet, desktop.
- Must respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Must maintain accessible contrast and visible focus states.
- The startup section's visual identity may be ambitious; its factual
  claims must remain conservative (see `RULES.md`).
